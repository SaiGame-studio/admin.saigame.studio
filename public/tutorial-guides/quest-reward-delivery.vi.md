By default, rewards are sent to the Mailbox. You can switch to direct delivery in the Settings tab.

## 1. Global Settings
These configurations will apply to **all quests** by default:

* **Mailbox Customization:** You can customize the **Title** and **Body** text for reward emails to match your game's theme.
* **Direct Delivery:** To simplify the player experience, you can choose to send rewards directly to the **Main Inventory**, bypassing the mailbox entirely.
![alt](/tutorial-guides/quest-reward-delivery/step-01.png)

---

## 2. Overriding Settings for Specific Quests
If you have a unique quest that needs to follow different rules (e.g., a special event quest that must use the mailbox while others use direct delivery):

1.  Create the **Quest Definition**.
2.  **Expand** the quest details.
3.  Select the **Override Game Settings** option to set custom reward logic for that specific quest only.
![alt](/tutorial-guides/quest-reward-delivery/step-02.png)

---
> **Developer Tip:** Use Direct Delivery for high-frequency daily quests to reduce friction, but keep the Mailbox for "Epic" story rewards to give them more impact.
